Chapter III.

The places we love.

Hand-picked by Michele & Ilaria after fifteen summers in the white city.

01.Dinner · Cave restaurant

Osteria del Tempo Perso

The most beloved address in the old town — set inside an ancient cave, dishes that taste of the sea and the orchards. Book a few days ahead.

“If it's your first night in Ostuni, this is the postcard.”

Open in Maps →
02.Nature · Marine reserve

Torre Guaceto

A protected coastline of turquoise water and dunes, twenty minutes by car. Take a bike, take time.

Open in Maps →
03.Day trip · UNESCO

I trulli di Alberobello

A short drive into the Itria Valley — cone-roofed houses, a UNESCO site that still feels lived-in.

Chapter V.

Hello, we're Michele & Ilaria.

Mi Casa was born from a love at first sight: walking through Ostuni's ancient white streets, the breathtaking view over the Itria Valley and the sea.

We renovated the apartment in 2022 to offer every modern comfort while keeping the soul of the white city. The name joins our two initials — and the idea of feeling at home, away from home.
