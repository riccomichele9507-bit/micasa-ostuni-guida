The places we love.

Hand-picked by Michele & Ilaria after fifteen summers in the white city.

01.Dinner · Cave restaurant

Osteria del Tempo Perso

The most beloved address in the old town — set inside an ancient cave, dishes that taste of the sea and the orchards. Book a few days ahead.

“If it's your first night in Ostuni, this is the postcard.”

02.Nature · Marine reserve

Torre Guaceto

A protected coastline of turquoise water and dunes, twenty minutes by car. Take a bike, take time.

I trulli di Alberobello

A short drive into the Itria Valley — cone-roofed houses, a UNESCO site that still feels lived-in.

Chapter VI.

Hello, we're Michele & Ilaria.

MiCasa was born from a land rich in culture and tradition, and from Michele and Ilaria's genuine love for hospitality — a place to relax, but also to discover the territory and the ancient Apulian tradition.

The name recalls hospitality and the feeling of being at home away from home — it joins the initials of the two owners. It is an apartment, completely renovated in 2022 to offer all the comforts of a modern home while keeping the tradition and typical taste of the White City.

Casa Bianca — Editorial. Serif typography (Fraunces), generous whitespace, photo-led storytelling. Suited to boutique B&Bs, design hotels and properties with a voice.
