## Hello, We're Michele & Ilaria

MiCasa was born from a land rich in culture and tradition and from Michele and Ilaria's genuine love for hospitality: a place to relax but also discover the territory and the ancient Apulian tradition. The name recalls hospitality and feeling at home away from home, and joins the initials of the two owners. It is an apartment, completely renovated in 2022 to have all the services of a modern home but maintaining the tradition and typical taste of the White City.

## How to reach MiCasa — From Bari Airport

1. Take highway **16**, direction Brindisi
2. Take the **Villanova** exit
3. Follow signs for **Ostuni** (written in blue)
4. Continue to **Ostuni center** (written in white), then continue for **Fasano** (start following Google Maps here)
5. Follow for **Bari / Corso Giuseppe Mazzini**
6. At the first turn, right onto **via G.C. Bovio**, go straight on
7. You will find **Vico Costa** on the left — and here is MiCasa
